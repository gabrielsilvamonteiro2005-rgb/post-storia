Privacy Policy

La Storia Sconosciuta Poster is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel and Instagram/TikTok profiles called "La Storia Sconosciuta" to publish short educational history content on the owner's own accounts.

What data is used

The tool uses an authorization token that the owner grants for the owner's own TikTok account, only to upload and publish videos created by the owner. The tool does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person. It does not read viewers' comments, followers or messages.

Where data is stored

The authorization token is stored only on the owner's computer, in a private configuration file. Nothing is sent to third parties other than TikTok itself, in order to publish the owner's videos.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services).
